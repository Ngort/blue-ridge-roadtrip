SOUTH CAROLINA · THE MEETING POINT

Charleston
Sunday · November 8

Meet around 2pm, collect the car, and begin the trip in the late afternoon. Give this first day a simple job: get everyone together and reach Greenville comfortably.

A SUGGESTED RHYTHM

Make the stop your own.

01

14:00 · Meet and settle

Allow time to meet, eat and collect luggage. The rental comparison assumes Charleston airport (CHS) pickup at 4pm; that is a planning slot, not a reservation.

02

Late afternoon · Head inland

Allow roughly 3½–4 hours of driving to Greenville, before stops or traffic. Expect an evening arrival and tell the hotel if you will be late. There is no daylight sightseeing commitment on this transfer day.

03

A different first night

Going directly to Asheville would mean a longer drive of roughly 4½–5½ hours. It creates a two-night Asheville option, but changes the first two days of this plan.

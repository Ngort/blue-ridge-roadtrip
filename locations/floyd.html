Floyd
Wednesday night → Thursday morning

Choose Floyd for a quieter final mountain night. If Thursday requires an early arrival in Roanoke, keep Floyd as a daytime stop and sleep in Roanoke instead.

THE FEEL OF THE PLACE

Atmosphere.

Floyd is a small crossroads town where craft shops, country cooking and Appalachian music sit close together. Its appeal is the slower pace and a few distinctive storefronts, rather than a long attraction checklist. Wednesday is quieter than the famous Friday music night, so arrive with realistic shop-hour expectations and a dinner choice that is actually open midweek.

A SUGGESTED RHYTHM

Make the stop your own.

01

On the way · Mabry Mill

Allow 30–60 minutes for the grounds and the approximately half-mile easy walk. Interior access is seasonal. The restaurant operator plans 2026 renovations and a spring 2027 opening, so bring food rather than relying on a restaurant stop here.

02

In town · Check the clock

The Floyd Country Store currently lists Wednesday shop hours of 11am–3pm. A later arrival may miss it. Do not plan dinner around those daytime shop hours.

03

Thursday · Roanoke

Budget roughly 1–1½ hours driving to Roanoke, plus any stops. The car plan assumes a provisional 4pm ROA return. An early commitment in Roanoke makes a Wednesday-night room there the better choice.

THE ROOM PLAN

Where to sleep

Hotel Floyd offers a central-town option at an estimated $130–190 per room; confirm breakfast and parking. Oak Haven Lodge is estimated at $115–155 and describes itself as less than five minutes from downtown; do not assume a convenient walk. EAT WELL, KEEP IT SIMPLE

A couple of good places to eat.

Casual choices for this stop, with restaurant information and independent reviews or local guides to help you choose. Published prices exclude extras unless noted; recheck hours before setting off.

El Charro Mexican Grill

A practical Wednesday dinner at 302 South Locust Street, beneath Dogtown. Mexican plates and à-la-carte items let you keep the bill controlled.

Budget: Restaurant-hosted menu lists cheese quesadilla $6.50 and grilled chicken quesadilla $9.99; tax, drinks and extras additional.

When to go: Restaurant ordering page lists Wednesday 11am–10pm; reconfirm close to travel.

Blue Ridge Diner

A downtown diner for breakfast or an early lunch. It is especially useful Thursday before leaving Floyd; it is not a Wednesday dinner solution.

Budget: Official menu: biscuits and gravy $6.99; Ridge Breakfast $8.74, before tax, drinks and tip.

When to go: Official Wednesday and Thursday hours: 6:30am–2pm. Closed Tuesday.

NINE STOPS + ONE PRACTICAL ALTERNATIVE

Get to know
each stop.

A guide for every proposed location, from the Charleston meeting point to the Roanoke finish. Choose a town for the suggested rhythm, practical room options and local events.

01 · The meeting point

Charleston ↗

Sunday · November 8

Meet around 2pm, collect the car, and begin the trip in the late afternoon. Give this first day a simple job: get everyone together and reach Greenville comfortably.

02 · Night one

Greenville ↗

Sunday night → Monday morning

An easy first landing after the late Charleston departure. Sleep here, then use Monday morning for a little riverside time before heading toward the mountains.

03 · Waterfall day stop

Brevard ↗

Monday · November 9

A small-town pause and one focused waterfall visit fit well between Greenville and Asheville. Keep the route compact so the stop does not consume the entire day.

04 · Night two

Asheville ↗

Monday night → Tuesday morning

Make Asheville the comfortable middle of the trip: a meal, a room, and a little city time after the waterfall. Resist adding every mountain stop to Monday.

05 · The mountain gateway

Linville ↗

Tuesday · November 10

Treat Linville as the transition from the Asheville transfer into a short scenic mountain afternoon. One well-chosen walk is enough before Blowing Rock.

06 · Mountain afternoon

Blowing Rock ↗

Tuesday · November 10

Pair a short walk with a free museum visit, then decide whether to stay around Blowing Rock or drive to a proposed room in Boone.

07 · The practical mountain base

Boone ↗

Tuesday night → Wednesday morning

Boone adds an economical room comparison, a free exhibition, and a genuinely dated evening recital to the High Country stop.

08 · Night four · small-town version

Floyd ↗

Wednesday night → Thursday morning

Choose Floyd for a quieter final mountain night. If Thursday requires an early arrival in Roanoke, keep Floyd as a daytime stop and sleep in Roanoke instead.

09 · The finish · or night four alternative

Roanoke ↗

Thursday · November 12

Keep the last day flexible around your actual arrival deadline and car return. Roanoke can be a Thursday museum-and-overlook finish, or a Wednesday overnight with live music.

10 · Practical fallback

Christiansburg ↗

Wednesday night · alternative

A highway hotel fallback if the Floyd and Roanoke options do not suit the final budget. This replaces the fourth night; it is not an additional stop in the base itinerary.

Four nights, with choices

The proposed nights are Greenville, Asheville, the Blowing Rock / Boone area, and Floyd. Roanoke replaces Floyd if Thursday starts early. Brevard and Linville are daytime stops. Christiansburg is a highway hotel fallback. Hotel prices remain estimates; no rooms, cars or tickets have been booked.
